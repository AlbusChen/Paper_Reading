Mental World Modeling HF Daily
Hao Fei, Yiran Zhao · 2026-07-29
cs.CLHF精选world modelsmental state modelinghuman decision predictionmultimodal reasoning
This paper argues that world models should represent hidden mental states, not only physical scene dynamics, to predict human decisions. It formulates Mental World Modeling as a framework coupling physical and mental world states, target-specific observations, and action-conditioned updates, and instantiates it in the training-free MENTIS baseline. Experiments on multimodal situated decision scenarios claim that explicit mental-state modeling is essential for predicting human behavior and reveal bottlenecks in current models.
中文摘要
本文认为，世界模型若要预测人类决策，不应只建模物理场景动态，还应表示信念、愿望、意图和社会可接受性等隐藏心理状态。作者提出Mental World Modeling框架，将物理状态与心理状态耦合，并根据目标个体的局部观察和候选动作进行联合更新，同时实现了无需训练的MENTIS基线。多模态情境决策实验表明，显式心理状态建模对预测人类行为至关重要，并揭示了当前模型的瓶颈。

Weak-to-Strong On-Policy Distillation HF Daily
Fangxu Yu, Zinan Lin, Xiaodong Liu, Weijia Xu et al. · 2026-07-28
cs.LGHF精选on-policy distillationweak-to-strong learningllm traininglogit contrast
This paper studies on-policy distillation for improving strong LLM students when all available supervision sources are weaker. It introduces W2S-OPD, which builds a proxy teacher in logit space from positive-negative weak model contrasts and distills the student on its own rollouts using reverse KL. Experiments across math and code benchmarks report that the student can outperform standard OPD and even surpass the domain teacher.
中文摘要
本文研究在所有监督来源都弱于学生模型时，如何通过在线策略蒸馏提升强LLM学生模型。作者提出W2S-OPD，通过正负弱模型的logit差异构造代理教师，并在学生自身 rollout 上用反向KL进行蒸馏。数学和代码基准实验表明，该方法优于标准OPD，并可使学生超过领域教师。

Zhoushun Yu, Xiaoyu Hu, Xiangyu Xu · 2026-07-30
cs.LGcs.AIcs.CVHF精选world modelslatent regularizationplanningquantile matching
This paper improves latent regularization for world models used in planning. It identifies that the Epps-Pulley objective in LeWorldModel gives vanishing corrective gradients for isolated tail samples, then proposes QQWorld, a quantile-quantile matching objective with cross-batch ranking to better align latents with Gaussian structure. Yifan Ding, Xincheng Wei, Yoshua Y. Li, Ziheng Li et al. · 2026-07-31
cs.LGcs.AIHF精选on-policy distillationrlvrtraining stabilityadvantage fusion
This paper studies how to combine reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards and on-policy distillation without destabilizing LLM training. It identifies entropy collapse caused by magnitude and temporal mismatches between RLVR and OPD advantages, then proposes SAF, which sparsifies, compresses, warms up, and anneals only the OPD advantage. Experiments on math and code benchmarks report more stable training and consistent gains over fixed-coefficient fusion.
中文摘要

Would You Walk to the Car Wash? Revealing the Salience Bias of Large Language Models in Commonsense Reasoning HF Daily
Zheng Wu, Chenhao Xue, Shijie Zheng, Yijie Lu et al. · 2026-07-30
cs.CLHF精选llm reasoningcommonsense reasoningsalience biasprompting
This paper investigates salience bias in large language models, where explicit but irrelevant distractors cause models to neglect implicit commonsense prerequisites. The authors introduce the SaliTrap Benchmark across four trap dimensions and evaluate 12 state-of-the-art LLMs, finding that mainstream models are broadly vulnerable and that trap detection can be separated from successful avoidance. They argue the failure is mainly knowledge suppression rather than knowledge absence, since context-free probing recovers much of the missing commonsense behavior, and show that lightweight inference-time prompting can reduce the gap without retraining.
中文摘要
本文研究大型语言模型在常识推理中的显著性偏差，即模型会被显式但无关的干扰条件吸引，从而忽略任务所需的隐含物理或常识前提。作者构建了覆盖四类陷阱维度的SaliTrap基准，并评估12个先进语言模型，发现主流模型普遍受此影响，且识别陷阱并不必然意味着能避免错误。论文认为该问题主要是知识被抑制而非知识缺失，因为去除误导性任务框架后的知识探测可恢复大量常识行为，并表明轻量级推理时提示无需重训即可缩小差距。

BANDMAS: Causality-Inspired Semantic Packet Scheduling for Bandwidth-Efficient Multi-Agent Collaboration ★ 高度相关
Jiangwen Dong, Wanyu Lin · 2026-08-01
cs.MAmulti-agent communicationbandwidth schedulingsemantic packetscoordination efficiencymulti-agentagent communicationinter-agent communication
This paper addresses the communication overhead of LLM-based multi-agent systems, where forwarding all messages increases traffic, latency, and token-processing cost. It proposes BANDMAS, which decomposes inter-agent messages into semantic packets and schedules transmission using causality-inspired replay valuation under bandwidth, latency, deadline, and context constraints. Experiments on SciFact, HotpotQA, and FanOutQA report substantially reduced application-layer bytes while achieving the best mean task metric among constrained methods.
中文摘要

In the Driver's Seat: A Multi-Company Study on the Reality of Autonomous Driving System Testing · 低相关
Qunying Song, Yuan Gao, Johannes Betz, Dietmar Pfahl et al. · 2026-07-17
cs.SEHF精选autonomous drivingsoftware testingscenario-based testingindustrial study
This paper studies current industrial practices and open challenges in autonomous driving system testing. The authors conduct interviews with experts from nine companies in six countries and use thematic analysis to synthesize testing approaches, limitations, possible solutions, and future trends. The study finds that scenario-based and X-in-the-loop testing dominate current practice, while realism, coverage, simulation fidelity, and acceptance criteria remain major unresolved challenges, motivating an evidence-centered closed-loop testing framework.
