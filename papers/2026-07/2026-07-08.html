cs.CVcs.AIcs.MMHF精选dense video captioningparallel decodingautoregressive modelsomni-modal understanding
This paper addresses the inefficiency of token-by-token autoregressive decoding for omni-modal dense video captioning. It proposes a parallelized autoregressive framework that restructures the causal dependency graph by exploiting weak dependencies across temporally distinct events while preserving sequential decoding within each event. Quantifying and Expanding the Theoretical Capacity of Late-Interaction Retrieval Models HF Daily
Julian Killingback, Varad Ingale, Hamed Zamani, Cameron Musco · 2026-07-07
cs.IRHF精选information retrievalmaxsimlate interactiontheory
This paper analyzes the theoretical capacity of late-interaction retrieval models using MaxSim, whose empirical strength is better understood than its representation power. It proves by construction that MaxSim can exactly replicate inner products for non-negative sparse vectors using compact representations, expresses some similarities unavailable to same-size vector inner products, and introduces Signed MaxSim for real-valued inner products. Empirical results on retrieval with negations support the theory, with Signed MaxSim improving out-of-domain performance over a standard MaxSim baseline in the reported settings.
