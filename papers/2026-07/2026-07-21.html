Comparative Study of Multi-Agent Actor-Critic Algorithms in Parameterized Action Reinforcement Learning ★ 高度相关
Ubayd Ali Bapoo, Clement N Nyirenda · 2026-07-21
cs.AIsingle-vs-multi agentactor-criticparameterized actionsscalabilitymulti-agentsingle-agentdecentralized
This paper studies whether single-agent actor-critic methods for parameterized action reinforcement learning benefit from shared-experience multi-agent extensions. It proposes MAGAC, MASAC, and MATQC, where independent agents share a replay buffer while retaining separate policies and value networks, and compares them with single-agent counterparts across multiple agent counts. The results indicate consistent gains for Greedy Actor-Critic, more modest gains for SAC and TQC variants, and a scalability trade-off as additional agents increase computation with limited extra benefit beyond five agents.
中文摘要
本文研究参数化动作强化学习中的单智能体演员-评论家方法是否能从共享经验的多智能体扩展中受益。论文提出MAGAC、MASAC和MATQC，使多个独立智能体共享回放缓冲区但保留各自的策略和值网络，并在不同智能体数量下与单智能体方法比较。结果表明，多智能体框架稳定提升Greedy Actor-Critic，但对SAC和TQC变体提升较有限，且超过五个智能体后额外收益有限而计算成本明显上升。
