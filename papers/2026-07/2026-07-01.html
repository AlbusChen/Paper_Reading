DOPD: Dual On-policy Distillation HF Daily
Xinlei Yu, Gen Li, Qingyi Si, Guibin Zhang et al. · 2026-06-29
cs.AIHF精选policy distillationprivileged informationllm training
This paper addresses failures in on-policy distillation caused by privileged information, which can create a privilege illusion between transferable capability gaps and non-transferable information asymmetry. It proposes DOPD, an advantage-aware dual distillation method that dynamically routes token-level supervision between privileged teacher and privileged student policies according to advantage gaps and relative probabilities. Experiments across LLM and VLM settings claim consistent improvements over vanilla OPD and other baselines, with additional gains in stability, robustness, continual learning, and out-of-distribution tasks.
中文摘要
本文研究在引入特权信息的在线策略蒸馏中出现的失败模式，即模型可能混淆可迁移能力差距与不可复制的信息不对称差距，形成“特权幻觉”。作者提出 DOPD，一种优势感知的双重蒸馏范式，根据优势差距和相对概率在特权教师与特权学生策略之间动态分配词元级监督。实验覆盖大语言模型和视觉语言模型设置，声称相较普通 OPD 及其他基线具有持续优势，并在稳定性、鲁棒性、持续学习和分布外任务上表现更好。
