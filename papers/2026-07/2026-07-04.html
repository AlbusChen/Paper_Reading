Social Networks of LLM Agents ★ 高度相关
Kaixuan Liu, Guojun Xiong, Weinan Zhang, Shengpu Tang · 2026-07-04
cs.LGmulti-agent llmssocial networksbelief aggregationherdingmulti-agent
This paper studies how populations of interacting LLM agents form collective beliefs and whether apparent consensus reflects knowledge aggregation or herding. It introduces SNLA, a framework that models effective influence among agents as a function of network position and attention sharpness, and shows theoretically and empirically that narrow attention can cause bounded effective sample size while wide attention can recover wisdom-of-crowds behavior under restrictive graph conditions.
中文摘要
本文研究相互作用的 LLM 智能体群体如何形成集体信念，以及表面共识究竟来自知识聚合还是羊群效应。作者提出 SNLA 框架，用智能体在网络中的位置和注意力集中程度来刻画实际影响力，并通过理论与实验表明，狭窄注意力会导致有效样本量受限，而宽注意力仅在特定无向且度正则图条件下恢复群体智慧。
