DeepLoop: Depth Scaling for Looped Transformers HF Daily
Shuzhen Li, Yifan Zhang, Jiacheng Guo, Quanquan Gu et al. · 2026-07-15
cs.LGcs.AIHF精选looped transformersresidual scalingrecurrent depthlanguage models
This paper studies residual scaling for looped Transformers, where a compact stack of physical blocks is reused over multiple rounds to increase unrolled depth without increasing stored parameters. It formalizes a tied-depth effect through a perturbation bound governed by a visit-alignment coefficient and derives DeepLoop scaling rules for recurrent depth. Experiments on GPT-style looped language models show neutral behavior without revisits and improved validation loss and downstream accuracy once recurrent depth is active.
中文摘要

It models the difference through an information bottleneck: single agents keep a full shared reasoning trace, while multi-agent systems communicate through bounded relay messages, creating a compression trade-off.
