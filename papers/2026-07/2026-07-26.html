Mingzhou Fan, Siyuan Xu, Mingxuan Yuan · 2026-07-26
cs.AIagent orchestrationgraph agentsresource allocationheterogeneous agentsmulti-agentagent coordination
This paper addresses resource allocation in graph-based multi-agent LLM systems, where static execution can waste computation on irrelevant components. Ionut Predoaia, Tuong Manh Vu, Konstantinos Barmpis, Dimitris Kolovos et al. · 2026-07-26
cs.MAinter-agent coordinationmcpa2allm agentsmulti-agentagent coordination
This paper compares MCP and A2A as protocols for inter-agent coordination in heterogeneous LLM-based agentic systems. Using an implementation-grounded software engineering scenario, it evaluates both protocols against requirements such as discoverability, multi-part messaging, asynchronous communication, observability, interoperability, and access control. The study finds that MCP offers a lighter coordination model with more application-layer responsibility, while A2A provides richer protocol-level support for stateful multi-turn coordination at higher implementation complexity.

Do LLM Debates Repeat Arguments Differently Across Languages? ★ 高度相关
Huiqian Lai · 2026-07-26
cs.CLllm debatemultilingual evaluationargument repetitionagent interaction
This paper studies whether LLM debate transcripts develop new argumentative content or repeat earlier claims across languages, using prior-argument similarity as an aggregate diagnostic over extracted argument units. In controlled eight-turn debates across 71 motions, six languages, and four model agents, Chinese shows a consistently higher similarity gap relative to English across multiple embedding models and robustness checks. A diversity-aware prompt reduces repetition overall but does not significantly close the Chinese-English gap, suggesting multilingual debate evaluation should assess argumentative development over time.
中文摘要
本文研究大语言模型辩论记录在不同语言中是否持续发展新的论证内容，或以不同措辞重复先前观点，并提出“先前论点相似度”作为基于抽取论点单元的总体诊断指标。在涵盖71个议题、六种语言和四个模型智能体的八轮受控辩论中，中文相较英文在多个多语言嵌入模型和稳健性检验下都表现出一致更高的相似度差距。多样性提示能降低各语言中的重复程度，但未显著缩小中英文差距，说明多语言辩论评估应关注论证随时间的发展。

The paper introduces Indic DiarBench, a multilingual benchmark for joint speaker diarization and ASR across all 22 scheduled Indian languages. The dataset contains natural multi-speaker audio from meetings, far-field recordings, and in-the-wild settings, with human-corrected time-aligned speaker-attributed transcripts. Baselines from commercial speech APIs and multimodal large language models are evaluated to support inclusive multilingual speech research.

A Frozen 12B Beats Frontier Models on Verified Work: 100% Accuracy, 0 Tokens, Bit-Exact, Forever · 低相关
Sietse Schelpe · 2026-07-26
cs.CLcs.AIcs.IRverified memoryfrozen llmsdeterministic reuseretrieval systems
This paper argues for augmenting frozen language models with a persistent memory of independently verified solutions, enabling deterministic zero-token reuse for previously solved problem families. The system stores only verified solutions and retrieves them for new instances, separating execution-bound capability from parameter scaling and contrasting exact addressing with approximate similarity retrieval. The reported results claim perfect reuse on selected fresh instances, consistency-gated open-ended reasoning acceptances, low-latency retrieval, and a large movable working-context store, while acknowledging that frontier models remain stronger at raw from-scratch reasoning.

Quoc-Huy Trinh, Lin Zhu, Sebastian Szyller · 2026-07-26
cs.CLcs.AIcontext attributionllm interpretabilityprovenancein-weight knowledge
The paper examines context attribution methods for LLMs when supplied context overlaps with knowledge already stored in model weights. It introduces an evaluation protocol with four metrics and a benchmark dataset with provenance labels to test whether methods can separate in-context from in-weight contributions. Experiments across four attribution methods show that they become unfaithful under such overlap and cannot reliably disentangle the two sources using contribution scores.
中文摘要
本文研究当输入上下文与模型权重中已有知识重叠时，LLM 上下文归因方法的可靠性问题。作者提出包含四个指标的评估协议，并构建带有来源标签的基准数据集，以检验方法能否区分上下文贡献和权重内知识贡献。实验表明，四种常见归因方法在存在重叠知识时会产生不忠实归因，且无法仅凭贡献分数可靠完成来源分离。

The paper addresses pathology image analysis, where diagnosis requires reasoning across multiple magnification scales and existing VLM benchmarks often allow shortcuts. It constructs PathScale-VQA with adversarial text-only screening and structure-controlled distractors, and trains PathScale-R1 using reasoning distillation followed by reinforcement learning with a scale-aware reward. Experiments report state-of-the-art cross-scale pathology reasoning and transfer to single-scale pathology VQA.

Baolei Li, Yiping Yuan, Yilin Zheng, Likang Yin et al. · 2026-07-26
cs.IRcs.AIcs.LGrecommendation systemssemantic idsembedding compressiongenerative retrieval
The paper targets memory and I/O bottlenecks in large-scale recommendation systems caused by massive dense embedding tables. It proposes Dual-purpose Semantic IDs, using hierarchical quantization to represent embeddings as discrete tokens that serve both collaborative identity modeling and on-the-fly content reconstruction through a lightweight decoder. Offline evaluation and production deployment indicate that this discrete representation can reduce storage and system overhead while supporting ranking and retrieval.

Maximum Satisfiability of Simple Temporal Problems · 低相关
Johannes K. Fichte, Johanna Groven, Peter Jonsson, Victor Lagerkvist et al. · 2026-07-26
cs.CCcs.AItemporal constraintsmaxstpparameterized complexityconstraint optimization
The paper studies MAXSTP, the problem of finding a maximum-cardinality consistent subset of constraints in Simple Temporal Problems. It analyzes parameterized complexity under variables, coefficient magnitude, treewidth, vertex cover size, and their combinations, showing several hardness results and giving algorithms for selected combined parameters. The results indicate that MAXSTP can be harder than related qualitative CSP optimization problems, though fixed-parameter tractability is possible under parameters such as coefficient magnitude plus vertex cover size.
中文摘要
本文研究简单时间问题中的 MAXSTP，即在可能不一致的时间约束集合中寻找最大一致子集。作者从变量数、系数大小、树宽、顶点覆盖数及其组合等参数出发分析参数化复杂性，给出多项困难性结论和若干算法。结果表明 MAXSTP 往往比相关定性约束满足优化问题更难，但在系数大小与顶点覆盖数等组合参数下仍可获得固定参数可解性。
