SeekJudge: A Practical Reward Framework for Reinforcement Learning in Computer-Use Agents ★ 高度相关
Yang Wan, Zhenhao Zhang, Jierui Wang, Linchao Zhu · 2026-07-25
cs.AIcomputer-use agentsmulti-agent judgingreinforcement learningtrajectory evaluation
This paper addresses reward evaluation for long-horizon computer-use agents, where rule-based trajectory judgment can become brittle and model-based judges have lagged behind. It proposes SeekJudge, a framework in which four role-specialized agents condense, ground, seek, and analyze trajectory evidence through an iterative Seek-Analyze loop, using a distilled shared backbone. The authors claim SeekJudge matches or exceeds native rule-based supervision for online reinforcement learning, provides step-level judgments, reduces cost, and improves reward-server efficiency.
中文摘要
本文研究长程图形界面计算机使用智能体的奖励评估问题，指出规则评估容易陈旧且难以对齐人类意图，而已有模型裁判仍存在性能差距。作者提出 SeekJudge，由 Condense、Ground、Seek 和 Analyze 四个角色专门化智能体通过 Seek-Analyze 循环判断轨迹是否完成指令，并使用蒸馏得到的共享骨干模型。结果声称该框架在在线强化学习中达到或超过原生规则监督，同时提供步骤级判断、降低成本并提升奖励服务器效率。
