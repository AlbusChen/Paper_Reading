Generative World Renderer at the Speed of Play HF Daily
Guixu Lin, Zheng-Hui Huang, Siqi Yang, Ming-Hsuan Yang et al. · 2026-07-21
cs.CVHF精选generative renderingworld modelingreal-time videointeractive simulation
This technical report addresses the computational cost that prevents the original AlayaRenderer generative world renderer from real-time interactive use. It introduces AlayaRenderer-Flash, a few-step autoregressive streaming renderer with lightweight distilled codecs that preserves G-buffer and text-prompt interfaces while enabling continuous rendering over unbounded input streams. The paper claims substantial inference-cost reduction while retaining core rendering capabilities, and demonstrates integration with a physics engine for a playable 30 FPS generative world.
