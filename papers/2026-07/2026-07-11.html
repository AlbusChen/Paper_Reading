Lingyun Shen, Xuejia Guo · 2026-07-11
cs.CLcs.AImulti-agent summarizationlong documentsquestioningllm agentsmulti-agent
This paper addresses long-document summarization with LLMs, where input length and content complexity challenge direct summarization. It proposes a stepwise questioning multi-agent framework in which expert and editor agents guide another agent by asking aspect-specific questions and giving revision clues. Experiments on two long-document scientific datasets using standard automatic metrics indicate that the method improves summarization effectiveness.
中文摘要

Temporary Authority, Permanent Effects: Commit-Time Authorization for LLM Agents ◆ 相关
Igor Santos-Grueiro · 2026-07-11
cs.CRcs.AIllm agentsauthorizationtool safetycommit boundarymulti-agent
This paper studies commit-time authorization for LLM agents, arguing that durable actions should only be committed when the authority evidence behind them remains fresh, causally prior, effect-bound, and eligible at commit time. It introduces a controlled-invalidation suite across browser, API/tool, and multi-agent workflows and shows that visible task success often masks unauthorized durable commits. The proposed CommitGuard boundary monitor blocks stale durable-effect attempts when runtimes expose the necessary witness and dependency signals.
中文摘要
