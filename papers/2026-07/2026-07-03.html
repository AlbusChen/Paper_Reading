Morphing into Hybrid Attention Models HF Daily
Disen Lan, Jianbin Zheng, Yuxi Ren, Xin Xia et al. · 2026-06-29
cs.CLHF精选hybrid attentionlong contextmodel conversion
This paper addresses how to choose which transformer layers should retain full attention when converting models into hybrid attention architectures. It formulates layer selection as a budget-constrained subset optimization problem and proposes FlashMorph, which learns layerwise gates in a morphable model using synthetic long-context retrieval data before discretizing the architecture. Experiments claim better hybrid configurations, preserved long-context and general performance, and reduced layer-selection cost.
中文摘要
本文研究将 Transformer 转换为混合注意力模型时，如何选择保留全注意力的层。作者将该问题形式化为预算约束下的子集优化，并提出 FlashMorph，通过在可变形模型中学习逐层门控，再按预算离散化得到混合架构。实验声称该方法能发现更有效的配置，在保持长上下文召回和通用性能的同时降低层选择成本。

This paper studies failure cascades in medical multimodal reasoning, where early reasoning errors often lead to incorrect clinical VQA answers. It proposes Medical Reasoning-aware Policy Optimization, an RL method with step-wise process rewards that penalize earlier invalid reasoning steps more strongly when the final answer is wrong. Scaling Laws for Grid-Based Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search in High Dimensions HF Daily
Matthew J Liu, Wei Hang Zheng, Vidhan Purohit, Siqi Xie et al. · 2026-07-01
cs.LGcs.AIHF精选approximate nearest neighborscaling lawsgrid searchhigh-dimensional embeddings
This paper analyzes scaling behavior for grid-based approximate nearest neighbor search, focusing on dataset size and dimensionality. It systematically evaluates a multiprobe grid algorithm and reports a dimensional-scaling crossover on GloVe embeddings, where the grid method maintains a roughly constant dimensional exponent while several graph-, tree-, and partitioning-based methods degrade in throughput. The results suggest that grid-based ANN methods may be competitive in high-dimensional or rebuild-heavy settings, with possible implications for efficient transformer cost analysis because self-attention can be formalized as ANN.
中文摘要
