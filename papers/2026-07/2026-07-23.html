Trace: A Taxonomy-Guided Environment for Multidomain Visual Reasoning HF Daily
Md Tanvirul Alam · 2026-07-22
cs.CVHF精选visual reasoningrlvrprocedural environmentvision-language models
The paper addresses the lack of broad, exactly verifiable, and reproducible training data for extending reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards to vision-language models. It introduces Trace, a taxonomy-guided procedural environment that separates scene generation from answer computation through scene grammars, executable task programs, and shared semantic state. The authors report that RLVR training on Trace instances improves macro-average performance on external visual reasoning benchmarks for Qwen2.5-VL models, suggesting transfer beyond generated distributions.
中文摘要
该论文针对将可验证奖励强化学习扩展到视觉语言模型时缺乏广泛、精确可验证且可复现训练数据的问题。作者提出 Trace，一个由分类体系引导的多领域视觉推理环境，通过场景语法、可执行任务程序和共享语义状态将视觉生成与答案计算分离。实验表明，在 Trace 实例上进行 RLVR 训练可提升 Qwen2.5-VL 模型在外部视觉推理基准上的宏平均表现，说明程序化训练具有一定分布外迁移能力。

Amritesh Banerjee · 2026-07-23
cs.MAcs.AImulti-agent communicationsemantic driftnetwork topologyspectral monitoringmulti-agentagent communicationcommunication efficiency
This paper analyzes communication topology risks in clinical multi-agent language model networks, challenging assumptions that scale-free or small-world structures are optimal for semantic communication. It maps uncertainty trajectories into a clinical embedding space and studies graph structures through variance, entropy, cosine similarity, and spectral properties. The paper claims that clustered or hub-centric topologies can amplify semantic drift and hallucinations, and proposes dynamic spectral monitoring with algebraic-connectivity constraints to improve global state diffusion.
