AskChem: Claim-Centered Infrastructure for Chemistry Literature Synthesis HF Daily
Bing Yan, Gregory Wolfe, Stefano Martiniani, Kyunghyun Cho · 2026-07-30
cs.CLcs.AIcs.IRHF精选literature synthesisclaim retrievalprovenancechemistry search
AskChem addresses chemistry literature synthesis, where users and AI agents must manually locate, verify, and combine findings across many papers. It converts papers into atomic provenance-carrying claims and provides faceted search, evidence graphs, living taxonomies, and API access over a shared claim store. Benchmark results indicate improved DOI resolvability and citation density when a GPT reader is grounded in AskChem.
中文摘要
AskChem面向化学文献综合中跨论文查找、验证和组装证据困难的问题，现有系统通常只返回排序后的文档列表。它将论文转换为带有来源和证据定位的原子化类型声明，并在共享声明库上提供分面检索、证据图、动态分类体系以及REST、SDK和MCP接口。基准结果表明，将GPT阅读器接入AskChem后，DOI可解析性和引用密度得到提升。

PhiZero: A World Model Built Around Physical Language HF Daily
Shuyao Shang, Yuqi Wang, Ruopeng Gao, Xu Chen et al. · 2026-07-30
cs.CVHF精选world modelsphysical languagevideo generationsimulation
PhiZero proposes a physical world model centered on physical language, a compact discrete representation of world-state transitions. Instead of predicting future videos directly in pixel space, it first reasons over future evolution as physical-language sequences and then renders those transitions into video. Experiments across generation and understanding benchmarks support its ability to model physically coherent evolution and enable interactive simulation, action-conditioned generation, and motion transfer.
中文摘要
PhiZero提出一种围绕“物理语言”构建的物理世界模型，物理语言是表示世界状态转移的紧凑离散表征。不同于直接在像素空间预测未来视频，该模型先以物理语言序列推断未来演化，再将这些转移渲染为视频。多项生成和理解实验表明，该方法能够建模物理一致的世界演化，并具备交互式世界建模、动作条件仿真和零样本运动迁移潜力。

Beacon examines agentic visual reasoning through whether multimodal models know when tools are needed and whether tool use actually improves hard cases without harming easy ones. The authors quantify Mode Adaptiveness and Tool Effect, finding that existing models often invoke tools poorly and lose gains on easy examples. Beacon uses necessity-aware rewards and hint-guided capability expansion during reinforcement learning to improve adaptive tool invocation and tool-induced performance.

Jiawei Xu, Minghui Liu, Juzheng Zhang, Tom Goldstein et al. · 2026-07-30
cs.LGHF精选self-distillationpolicy optimizationreasoning modelskl regularization
β-OPSD analyzes on-policy self-distillation for reasoning language models as a member of a broader policy-optimization family controlled by a KL weight β. The paper derives the optimal policy as a geometric interpolation between a reference policy and privileged teacher, then implements the closed-form target through token-level logit mixing instead of costly reinforcement learning. Experiments on mathematical reasoning benchmarks show improved stability and downstream performance over vanilla OPSD.
中文摘要
β-OPSD将推理语言模型中的on-policy self-distillation解释为由KL权重β控制的更广义策略优化族的一员。论文推导出最优策略是参考策略与特权教师之间的几何插值，并用token级logit混合来实现闭式蒸馏目标，从而避免昂贵且高方差的强化学习优化。数学推理基准实验显示，该方法比普通OPSD具有更好的优化稳定性和下游推理性能。

Cheng Luo, Zefan Cai, Junjie Hu · 2026-07-22
cs.AIHF精选transformer architectureattention residualsscalinglanguage models
Multi-Head Attention Residuals addresses the limitation that attention residuals read depth history through a single distribution shared across all feature subspaces. MHAR reshapes the routing query into multiple per-subspace heads, allowing separate softmaxes over depth history with no added parameters and negligible compute. Training results across model scales show validation-loss improvements and support subspace disagreement as the mechanism behind the gains.
中文摘要
Multi-Head Attention Residuals研究注意力残差中所有特征子空间共享单一深度历史读取分布所带来的限制。MHAR将路由查询重塑为多个按子空间划分的头，使每个头对深度历史拥有独立softmax，同时不增加参数且计算开销很小。不同规模模型的训练结果显示验证损失下降，并通过探针支持子空间读取偏好分歧是收益来源。

cs.CVHF精选omnimodal llmstoken compressionaudio-video inferencemultimodal salience
The paper addresses token compression for omnimodal large language models, arguing that audio and video relevance often peak at different moments and that cross-modal guidance can discard critical cues. It proposes OmniScope, a training-free framework that uses the query as a shared semantic anchor while estimating audio and video salience separately, allocating modality-specific budgets and applying distinct pruning or merging strategies. Across audio-video benchmarks and Qwen2.5-Omni scales, the method claims the best average accuracy under compression and improved prefill speed and memory use with minimal accuracy loss at 25% token retention.

MPIE-Bench evaluates multi-person image editing, especially shared contact actions where current models produce anatomical and geometric failures. The benchmark contains video-mined editing triplets across scenes, interaction categories, and contact densities, and MPIE-Eval uses public multi-person mesh reconstruction to score anatomy and interaction geometry. Across ten editors, no model is strong on both axes, and the proposed metrics align better with human judgments than VLM checklist judges.

ACE-Data-0 addresses the data bottleneck in embodied intelligence by capturing synchronized human perception, motion, manipulation, object state, audio, and touch during goal-directed activity. The Ambient Capture Engine records table-scale and room-scale interactions in calibrated home environments, producing a large multisensory dataset across task categories and episodes.
