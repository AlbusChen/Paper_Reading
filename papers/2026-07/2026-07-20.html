The paper analyzes cooperative multi-agent policy optimization and asks how neighboring agents should be aggregated in PPO-style MARL objectives. It formalizes aggregation in the advantage and in the likelihood ratio using support matrices, proving that the expected objective depends on them only through their matrix product while variance behaves differently across the two axes. It introduces BRIM, a hardware-software co-designed accelerator combining Cyclic-Balanced Pruning with Pairwise Slot Donation to balance offline and runtime workloads. This paper presents Open Ant, a physical robot platform and corresponding simulation designed to bridge reinforcement learning research from simulated environments to real hardware. The platform is modeled after the Gymnasium Ant environment and supports learning walking policies directly on the robot as well as sim-to-real transfer. This article develops digital statecraft as a conceptual foundation for governing digital systems and governing with algorithmic systems. It distinguishes statecraft over digital infrastructure from statecraft with digital tools and argues that technical coherence and legitimate authority are both necessary and in tension.
