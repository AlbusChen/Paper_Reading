This paper introduces Spatial-IQ, a hierarchical diagnostic benchmark for decomposing spatial intelligence in multimodal large language models. It breaks object counting in stacked 3D structures into perceptual and cognitive sub-tasks, generates a large synthetic dataset with ground truth, and evaluates models across text, multiple-choice image, and image-editing formats.
