SAGE: A Socially-Aware Generative Engine for Heterogeneous Multi-Agent Navigation ★ 高度相关
Lan Hu, Minghui Liwang, Wenbo Zhu, Xinlei Yi et al. · 2026-07-18
cs.ROcs.MAheterogeneous multi-agent navigationsocially aware planningdiffusion trajectory generationrobot coordinationmulti-agentheterogeneous multi-agent
SAGE addresses safe and socially compliant navigation in heterogeneous human-robot multi-agent environments where interactions are asymmetric and social norms are soft constraints. It uses a directed heterogeneous graph with a Heterogeneous Graph Transformer, a diffusion-based trajectory and planning generator, and training-free safety-social energy guidance at inference. Experiments on real and synthetic datasets report improved safety and social compliance while maintaining task performance, with scalability to teams of up to 20 robots.
中文摘要
SAGE研究异构人机多智能体环境中的安全且符合社会规范的导航问题，重点处理非对称交互和软性社会约束。该方法用有向异构图和异构图Transformer编码不同类型参与者的交互，并结合扩散式轨迹生成与推理阶段的安全-社会能量引导。实验表明，SAGE在真实和合成数据集上能提高安全性与社会合规性，同时保持任务表现，并可扩展到最多20个机器人团队。

cs.SEmulti-agent llmsmodel-driven engineeringformal verificationtraceabilitymulti-agentsingle-agent
RADIANT targets traceable model-driven system development, especially for safety-critical systems, where heterogeneous engineering artifacts complicate maintenance and change management. It combines MDE with multi-agent LLM decomposition to generate requirement-linked models, executable traceability, change-impact analysis, and formally verified CSP behavior models with counterexample-driven repair. This paper studies non-uniform scaling formation control in open multi-agent systems where agents, edges, and leaders can change dynamically. It proposes distributed protocols that adjust partial Laplacian weights to preserve the desired formation spectrum under topology changes such as joining, leaving, edge addition, edge removal, and leader reassignment.
