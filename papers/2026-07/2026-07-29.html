ReDesign: Recovering Editable Design Structures from Images via Agentic Decomposition HF Daily
Jooyeol Yun, Jintae Park, Hyesu Lim, Junha Hyung et al. · 2026-07-28
cs.CVHF精选agentic decompositiondesign reconstructiontool useeditable layers
ReDesign addresses the problem of reconstructing editable design files from raster images, where editability requires recovering typography, vector geometry, colors, grouping, and layer ordering. It proposes an agentic framework that incrementally builds a layer hierarchy by selecting specialized multimodal tools, with local verification to accept, prune, or retry steps. Zichao Lin, Yifeng Xie, Bowen Qu, Haiming Wang et al. · 2026-07-27
cs.CVHF精选multimodal evaluationvisual perceptionbenchmarkingmllms
The paper introduces PerceptionBench, a benchmark designed to isolate atomic visual perception capabilities in multimodal large language models rather than conflating perception with reasoning or domain knowledge. It derives a taxonomy from failure points across existing benchmarks and constructs 3,000 verified short-answer questions covering ten perceptual capabilities. The paper proposes Projection Pursuit CPCANet for domain generalization, addressing rank-deficient covariance estimation in mini-batch CPCA-based geometric alignment methods.
