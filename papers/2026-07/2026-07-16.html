Registers Matter for Pixel-Space Diffusion Transformers HF Daily
Nikita Starodubcev, Ilia Sudakov, Ilya Drobyshevskiy, Artem Babenko et al. · 2026-05-15
cs.CVHF精选diffusion transformersregister tokenspixel-space generationfeature maps
This paper studies whether register tokens, known to mitigate high-norm patch-token outliers in Vision Transformers, are useful for pixel-space Diffusion Transformers. It finds that DiTs differ from ViTs because they do not exhibit such patch-token outliers, yet registers still improve pixel-space DiTs, especially by producing cleaner high-noise feature maps. The authors further propose Register Guidance to amplify the contribution of helpful register tokens for better visual structure and coherence.
中文摘要
本文研究在视觉 Transformer 中用于缓解高范数 patch-token 离群问题的 register token 是否也适用于像素空间扩散 Transformer。作者发现 DiT 与 ViT 不同，并不存在相同的 patch-token 离群现象，但 register 仍能改善像素空间 DiT，尤其是在高噪声阶段产生更干净的特征图。基于这些观察，论文提出 Register Guidance，以增强有助于视觉结构和一致性的 register token 贡献。
