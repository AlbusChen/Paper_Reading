Yihao Chen, Shi Chang, Khaled Chawa, Feng Lin et al. · 2026-07-29

MindForge targets the difficulty of training coding agents to synthesize complete programs from scratch across the full software engineering life cycle. It automatically converts open-source command-line programs into source-free environments exposing only a compiled executable and documentation, then uses teacher-agent trajectories to fine-tune a smaller language model. The resulting model improves substantially on ProgramBench and across seven unseen software engineering benchmarks, suggesting that scalable source-free training environments can strengthen whole-program synthesis capabilities.
中文摘要

SpecFirst: Behavioral Specification Elicitation as a First-Class Step in Agent-Based Program Synthesis from Scratch HF Daily
Yihao Chen, Shi Chang, Feng Lin, Khaled Chawa et al. · 2026-07-29
cs.SEcs.CLHF精选coding agentsspecification elicitationprogram synthesisagent decomposition
SpecFirst studies from-scratch program synthesis by LLM-based agents under sparse behavioral information from documentation and an execute-only binary. It decomposes the task into two phases: a specification agent first probes the binary and converts observations plus documentation into a structured behavioral specification, then a code synthesis agent implements against that stable reference. Across ProgramBench instances and multiple models, this staged requirements-engineering approach improves pass rates and exploration coverage over a single-loop baseline.
中文摘要

Peter Kirgis, Sayash Kapoor, Andrew Schwartz, Stephan Rabanser et al. · 2026-07-29
cs.AIcs.CYcs.LGHF精选ai research agentsopen-ended evaluationr&d automationagent failure modes
This paper evaluates whether frontier AI agents can conduct open-ended AI research, using shadow evaluations in which agents attempt the central research question of unpublished papers and original authors grade the results. In two NeurIPS 2026 case studies, agents completed engineering work over six days but failed to make substantial progress on the research questions, leading authors to reject both outputs. The analysis identifies recurring failures in research judgment, creativity, backtracking, resource awareness, and instruction adherence.
中文摘要

It uses an evolutionary search framework in which an LLM proposes reformulations that are verified and benchmarked, and introduces Profile-Diverse Retention to keep behaviorally diverse prior attempts using runtime-vector diversity. Experiments on CSPLib problems show that iterative reformulation can produce held-out speedups, with diverse-context retention and validation-based selection improving performance.

This paper addresses industrial online 3D bin packing, where placement quality depends not only on policy learning but also on candidate generation and representation. It proposes OPAL, combining an operationally guided empty-maximal-space generator, operational candidate representations, and a masked PPO-trained ranking policy with an xLSTM-based placement encoder. On the BED-BPP benchmark, OPAL improves space utilization and shows that both operational candidate generation and learned ranking contribute to performance.

This paper addresses the cost of collecting diverse egocentric manipulation data for embodied AI. It presents EgoGenesis, an egocentric world-action simulator built on a pretrained video generation prior with Online Anchored Projective Memory for geometric consistency and Action-3D RoPE for camera-aware action conditioning. The generated trajectories improve downstream real-robot generalization when used to augment limited real trajectories.
