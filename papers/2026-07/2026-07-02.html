This paper addresses routing in prefill-decode disaggregated serving for mixture-of-experts LLMs, where load-balanced decode workers can still differ in latency due to expert activation locality. Shijie Li, Yilin Gao, Siyuan Yang, Tieyuan Chen et al. · 2026-07-01
cs.CVHF精选multimodal reasoninglatent reasoningvariational learninganswer leakage
This paper studies continuous latent reasoning in multimodal large language models, focusing on the train-inference mismatch caused by training-time posteriors conditioned on ground-truth answers. It proposes Asymmetric Mutual Variational Learning, using forward KL to align the prior with the posterior and reverse KL to regularize the posterior away from answer-leaking regions. Theoretical analysis and experiments in a latent-integrated MLLM show improved multimodal reasoning performance and latent-space stability over discrete and latent-reasoning baselines.
中文摘要
