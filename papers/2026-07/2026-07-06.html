The Mirage of Optimizing Training Policies: Monotonic Inference Policies as the Real Objective for LLM Reinforcement Learning HF Daily
Jing Liang, Hongyao Tang, Yi Ma, Yancheng He et al. · 2026-06-28
cs.LGHF精选llm reinforcement learningtraining-inference mismatchpolicy optimizationpost-training
This paper examines reinforcement learning instability in LLM post-training caused by mismatch between training and inference engines. It argues that improving the training policy does not necessarily improve the deployed inference policy, and proposes Monotonic Inference Policy Improvement with a two-step MIPU framework that constructs sampler-referenced candidate updates and selectively accepts them using an inference-side gap proxy. Experiments under high mismatch show improved reasoning performance and training stability.
中文摘要
本文研究大语言模型强化学习后训练中由训练引擎与推理引擎不匹配导致的不稳定性。作者指出，训练策略的改进并不必然带来部署时推理策略的改进，并提出 Monotonic Inference Policy Improvement，以及两步式 MIPU 框架，通过采样器参照的候选更新和推理侧差距代理选择性接受更新。在高不匹配条件下的实验显示，该方法提升了推理表现和训练稳定性。

This paper introduces DataComp for VLMs, a benchmark for controlled data-centric experiments in vision-language model training. It collects 160 datasets across image-caption, interleaved multimodal documents, text-only data, and instruction-tuning data, enabling evaluation of filtering, mixing, formatting, and sampling strategies across model and token scales.
